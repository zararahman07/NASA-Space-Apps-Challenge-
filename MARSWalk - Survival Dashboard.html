🧑‍🚀 HUMAN HABITABILITY
LETHAL / ZERO

High $CO_2$ and near-vacuum pressure (~0.6% Earth sea-level) will cause rapid hypoxia and ebullism without a pressurized suit/habitat.

🌱 ISRU / RESOURCE VALUE
EXCELLENT / HIGH

Abundant $CO_2$ is prime feedstock for MOXIE oxygen generation, Sabatier methane rocket fuel synthesis, and greenhouse plant carbon fixation.

NASA MOXIE Principle: The Mars Oxygen ISRU Experiment converts atmospheric $CO_2$ into breathable $O_2$ and $CO$ via solid oxide electrolysis at ~800°C.

Martian Settlement Operations Manual

Standard operating protocols for life support, radiation mitigation, and long-term agricultural sustainability.

01. FIRST 24 HOURS

Establish habitat pressurization (1 atm, 21% O2 / 78% N2 blend), verify thermal loop, and connect surface solar panels to battery reserves.

02. WATER & ICE HARVESTING

Deploy automated excavation rovers to sub-surface ice sheets. Thermal melter wells heat regolith ice into liquid phase for closed-loop filtration.

03. BIOSPHERE AGRICULTURE

Utilize closed hydroponic channels. Leach toxic perchlorates from local soil if using regolith substrate, and supplement photoperiod with LED growth spectrums.

EVA HAZARD RESPONSE (triggered by the map's advisory banner)

⚠ Radiation spike at a site

Abort EVA toward that site, return to the habitat's shielded module, and hold until the dose rate drops below the safe threshold.

Data trigger: MAVEN SEP instrument
⚠ Dust storm approaching

Seal suit vents, cut reliance on solar-forecast tasks, and move to low ground away from crater rims to limit wind-driven abrasion.

Data trigger: MRO MARCI daily imaging
⚠ Rough terrain on route (red line)

Reduce traverse speed, prefer the green safe route where one exists, and keep a wide turning radius near slopes over 15°.

Data trigger: MOLA / HiRISE slope rasters
⚠ Sudden pressure/temperature swing

Check suit seals and thermal regulation before continuing — sharp swings often precede local dust activity.

Data trigger: Curiosity REMS / Perseverance MEDA
