Science objective. Study the boxwork: a network of resistant ridges (~5 m wide) enclosing 10–20 m hollows on Mount Sharp, thought to record groundwater flowing through rock cracks. Curiosity explored it from sol 4534 (May 2025) to about sol 4844.
Layered data: topography (MOLA + rover elevation) · optical (Viking, rover photos) · geophysical (rover tilt) · chemical/spectral (CheMin, ChemCam findings per stop) · radiation (RAD baseline + live DONKI) · atmosphere/thermal (computed from Mars24). What you will see: select a stop

Atmosphere at Gale NASA SAM, embedded

Mean mixing ratios from Curiosity's SAM instrument (Trainer et al., 2019). Pressure is under 1% of Earth sea level: suits are mandatory.

Radiation NASA RAD, embedded

Average surface dose equivalent at Gale: 0.67 mSv per day (absorbed 0.210 ± 0.040 mGy/day), measured by Curiosity's RAD (Hassler et al., 2014). The cruise to Mars was 1.8 mSv/day. The plan converts this to a dose per EVA; suit shielding and solar storms are not included.
